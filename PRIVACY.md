# Privacy

Hearth is a code editor that runs AI models on your own computer. This page explains what Hearth does with your information. It applies to every edition of Hearth: the one from GitHub and the one from the Mac App Store.

## What Hearth collects

Nothing. Hearth has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. Its makers never receive your code, your requests, your files, or anything about how you use Hearth.

## What stays on your computer

Your projects, your conversations, the images you attach, the index for search by meaning and the models you download are kept on your computer, in Hearth's data folder. Deleting that folder, or uninstalling Hearth, removes them.

## When Hearth uses the network

Hearth connects to other computers only in these cases, and only when you ask for them:

- **Downloading a model.** When you click to download a model, Hearth fetches it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and the servers it uses for downloads). Hugging Face receives an ordinary download request, which includes your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies.
- **Model servers you choose.** If you use Ollama, LM Studio or another model server, Hearth sends it your requests and the code they need. These servers normally run on your own computer. If you add one that runs elsewhere, Hearth sends that data there.
- **Checking for updates.** The edition from GitHub asks GitHub for the latest version when you click Check for Updates. The Mac App Store edition does not: the App Store updates it.
- **Links.** Links you click open in your web browser.

Hearth's built-in model engine only accepts connections from your own computer, and only from Hearth.

## Children

Hearth collects no data from anyone, children included.
